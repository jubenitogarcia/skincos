# Rights, consent, and originality

References may inform permitted structural, rhythmic, timbral, vocal, or mix
characteristics. They never authorize copying melody, lyrics, hooks, a living
artist's recognizable identity, or a voice.

Inputs record rights status, use scope and source URI. `RESTRICTED` fails closed.
Vocal work requires `voice_consent.status=GRANTED`; output records voice ID and
synthetic-voice disclosure. Missing/denied consent blocks work. MSC-90 scores
originality and rejects high similarity; review means targeted regeneration,
not forced approval. Logs contain sanitized metadata only.
